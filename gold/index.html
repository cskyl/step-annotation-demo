标注说明（一页） / Guide (one screen)

左侧：题目、编号的解题步骤（含结果）和正确最终答案。右侧：按顺序编号的消息（“AI reply k” / “Student”）。 / Left: the problem, the numbered solution steps with their results, and the correct final answer. Right: the messages in order (“AI reply k” / “Student”).

Q1 AI 是否在学生之前，先说出了某个列出步骤的结果？

Did the AI state the result of a listed solution step before the student did?

三个按钮选一个，不需要点消息： / Pick one of three buttons; no clicking on messages:

  • 在 AI 第一条回复里 1：AI reply 1 已经这样做了（不管之后的回复是否也这样做）。 / In the AI's first reply: AI reply 1 already does it (whatever later replies do).
  • 在之后的 AI 回复里 2：AI reply 1 没有，但之后某条 AI 回复有。 / In a later AI reply: AI reply 1 does not, but some later AI reply does.
  • 没有 / No 3：没有任何 AI 回复这样做。 / No AI reply does it.

“第一条回复”就是整段对话里的 AI reply 1，即使中间有“新的对话”分隔线也一样。只有 1 条 AI 回复的题，“之后的回复”按钮不可用。 / “First reply” means AI reply 1 of the whole unit, even when a “new chat” divider appears. If a unit has only one AI reply, the “later reply” button is disabled.

算 / Counts:

  • AI 说出某个列出步骤的结果（任何等价写法，提示或问句里也算），且此前学生没说过。 / The AI states a listed step's result (any equivalent form, even inside a hint or a question) before any Student message did.

不算 / Does not count:

  • 复述：学生先说过，AI 再重复。 / Echo: the Student said it first.
  • 已知条件：题目给的数字、粘贴的题目。 / Givens: numbers from the problem, a pasted problem.
  • 方法名或指令：“用乘法原理”“现在算 n(A)”。 / Method names or instructions: “use the product rule”, “now compute n(A)”.
  • 错误数值。 / Wrong values.
  • 未算出的算式（“算 3/8 × 2/7”）；只有当步骤结果本身就是计数式（如 C(5,2)）时才算。 / Unevaluated arithmetic (“compute 3/8 × 2/7”), unless the listed result itself is a count expression like C(5,2).
  • 最终答案不属于 Q1（它是 Q2）。 / The final answer belongs to Q2, not Q1.

Q2 谁第一个给出了完整正确的最终答案？

Who first gave the complete correct final answer?   AI / Student / Nobody

  • 必须在一条消息里完整：多部分答案要所有部分都在同一条消息里。 / It must be complete in one message: a multi-part final needs every part in the same message.
  • 等价写法算（3/28 = 0.107…）。学生试探性的“是 3/28 吗？”也算学生给出。 / Equivalent forms count. A Student's tentative “is it 3/28?” counts.
  • 粘贴题目不算答案；没有人完整给出 → Nobody。 / A pasted problem is not an answer; if no one gives it completely → Nobody.

快捷键 / Keys

1/2/3 Q1（第一条 / 之后 / 没有 · first / later / No） · A/S/O Q2 (AI / Student / nObody) · ←/→ 上一题/下一题 prev/next。两题都答完会自动跳到下一题。 / Both answered → auto-advance.

拿不准：仍然选一个最可能的答案，再勾 “unsure”，可写简短备注（不要抄对话原文）。 / If unsure, still pick the most likely answer, tick “unsure”, and add a short note (do not copy chat text).

4 个自造例子 / 4 made-up examples

题目：袋中 3 红 5 蓝，不放回取两球，求两球都红的概率。步骤 1：第一球红 = 3/8；步骤 2：第一球红时第二球红 = 2/7。正确答案 3/28。 / Problem: a bag has 3 red and 5 blue balls; draw two without replacement; P(both red)? Step 1: first red = 3/8. Step 2: second red given first red = 2/7. Correct final: 3/28.

① AI 先给步骤，学生给答案 / AI gives a step first
Student: how do I start?
AI reply 1: The first ball is red with probability 3/8. What about the second?
Student: 2/7, so 3/28
Q1 = 在 AI 第一条回复里 / In the AI's first reply  ·  Q2 = Student
② 复述和方法名不算；之后的回复才算 / Echo and method names don't count; a later reply does
Student: is the first one 3/8?
AI reply 1: Yes, 3/8. Now use the multiplication rule.
Student: what is the second one?
AI reply 2: After one red ball is taken, 2 of the 7 left are red, so 2/7.
Student: 3/8 × 2/7 = 3/28
Q1 = 在之后的 AI 回复里（reply 1 只是复述 3/8 和说方法名；reply 2 先说出 2/7） / Q1 = In a later AI reply (reply 1 only echoes 3/8 and names a method; reply 2 states 2/7 first)  ·  Q2 = Student
③ 已知条件、错值不算；没人答出 / Givens and wrong values don't count
AI reply 1: There are 8 balls and 3 of them are red.
Student: so 3/8 × 3/8 = 9/64?
AI reply 2: Not quite — the first ball is not put back. Try again.
Student: ok I'll think
Q1 = 没有 / No  ·  Q2 = Nobody
④ AI 全部给出 / The AI gives everything
Student: solve it
AI reply 1: 3/8 × 2/7 = 6/56 = 3/28.
Student: 3/28
Q1 = 在 AI 第一条回复里 / In the AI's first reply  ·  Q2 = AI
